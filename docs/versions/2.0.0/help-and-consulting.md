# Help and Consulting

RuleBox is built by [Ortus Solutions](https://www.ortussolutions.com/), the team
behind ColdBox and BoxLang. If your team needs help adopting RuleBox or solving
a specific challenge, Ortus offers professional support and consulting.

## How We Can Help

Consulting can help your team with work such as:

- Designing rulebooks and organizing business rules for your application.
- Integrating RuleBox into a BoxLang or ColdBox application.
- Choosing an approach for managing rules with JSON, YAML, or a database.
- Using the audit trail, dry runs, Visualizer, and metrics to understand rule execution.
- Troubleshooting, testing, and improving an existing RuleBox implementation.

## Contact Ortus

Email [consulting@ortussolutions.com](mailto:consulting@ortussolutions.com?subject=RuleBox%20Professional%20Support)
to discuss your project and the support you need.

To help the team understand your request, include your BoxLang and ColdBox
versions, what you are trying to accomplish, and any relevant rulebook
configuration or error details. Remove credentials and sensitive data before
sharing logs or code.
